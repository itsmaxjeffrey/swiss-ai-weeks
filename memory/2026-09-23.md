## Lessons

- GLEIF API: page-number pagination hard-caps at 10,000 results (HTTP 400,
  `page[number] * page[size] <= 10000`) even when `meta.pagination.lastPage`
  reports more — full country slices need cursor pagination
  (`page[cursor]=*`, then follow `links.next`). `filter[entity.legalAddress.region]`
  is not an allowed filter on lei-records. Cached pages resume by reading the
  next cursor out of the previous page's `links.next`.
- pandas 3.x: `Series.map` on mixed str/None columns coerces None→NaN;
  keep date/parse helpers type-defensive.
- Feed formats bite: URLhaus puts its CSV header in a `#` comment line —
  always inspect raw file heads before writing parsers.
- Pipe background exec through `tail` and you lose live progress in `process
  poll` (tail buffers until exit) — poll the raw session instead.
